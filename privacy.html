Privacy Policy

Around the Block · Last updated: July 24, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, and no tracking. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, or photos.

How the app works

All star and planet data is bundled inside the app and works fully offline. The app makes no data requests to our servers. If you tap an external link (for example to NASA.gov), it opens in your browser and is governed by that site's own policies.

Where your data lives

Favorites and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent anywhere. Sky postcards you create are composed on your device and shared only through the system share sheet, at your direction.

Your choices

Everything works without an account. You can remove favorites at any time in the app, or clear all local data by deleting the app.

Children's privacy

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@aroundtheblock.space
